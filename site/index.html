Opening it the first time

  1. Open the ZIP, drag Magnetite to Applications, then double-click it.
  2. macOS says it can’t verify Magnetite. That’s expected for a free app that isn’t in Apple’s paid programme. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security. Click Open Anyway next to Magnetite, then confirm with Touch ID or your password.
  4. Look for the M in the menu bar. Play something in Spotify or Music and hover the notch. Allow the two permission prompts: one to control Spotify or Music, one to record system audio for the visualiser.

You only do this once. Only open a download you trust.

Prefer to skip the warning? Paste this into Terminal (Spotlight → Terminal):

curl -fsSL https://magnetite.app/install.sh | sh

The checksum and signature confirm the download is intact; Apple hasn’t reviewed it.

Using it

Open Spotify or Music and play a track on this Mac. Hover the notch to open Magnetite; move away to tuck it back into the menu bar.

The M menu holds your music source, gestures, notch calibration, launch at login and Quit.

Before you press play

macOS says it can’t open Magnetite

That’s expected on the first launch: Magnetite isn’t notarised by Apple. Follow the first-launch steps above, or use the Terminal line in the download section. Right-click → Open no longer works on macOS 15 and later.

What permissions does it need?

Automation lets Magnetite read and control Spotify or Music through Apple Events. System audio recording drives the visualiser: audio is processed in memory, never saved or uploaded. Both permissions live in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

No Magnetite account or tracking. Cover art may be downloaded from your player's artwork host, and synced lyrics are looked up on LRCLIB by track title and artist. Read the privacy details.

Do I have to connect a Spotify account?

Not for everyday listening. Play, pause, skip, seek and the liquid work with the Spotify desktop app, and Apple Music needs nothing extra, favourites included.

Liked songs, shuffle and repeat need a free Spotify developer app of your own. It takes a few minutes: the steps are in the README. Then choose “Connect Spotify Library…” in the M menu.

Will it work without a notch?

Yes. Displays without a notch get a floating pill. You still need macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon.

Why is there no track or moving liquid?

Start a track in Spotify or Music on this Mac and check the source in the M menu. Allow Magnetite under Automation for playback, and Screen & System Audio Recording for the visualiser, then quit and reopen it. Those permissions are separate.

How do I update or uninstall?

Rerun the installer to update, or replace the app with the latest official release. To remove it, disconnect Spotify if connected, quit Magnetite from the M menu, and delete the app from Applications.
