# magnetite.app redesign brief (2026-10-29)

## What the live site does that is generic
Swap test against Alcove, boring.notch, NotchNook-style pages (captured at 1280 and 390):
centered giant slogan ("Music, magnetized."), a download pill, a film of the app,
three gesture chips, a download block, an FAQ accordion. Any notch app could wear it.
TE OP-1 and Nova show the alternative: the product as an object, specific words, a
structure that only fits this product.

## Principles (each build choice cites one)
1. The liquid is the only spectacle. No other effect moves on its own.
2. Show the real app: the cut-out player, the screen film, the sim ported from the app, the real songs.
3. Sound is opt-in and offered up front, next to the first headline.
4. One primary action, "Download for Mac", always one click away.
5. The notch is the stage: the player hangs from the top of the page.
6. Gatekeeper steps are honest and calm: what macOS says, what to click, once.
7. Motion follows the liquid rules: out over 100-150 ms, eased drain over ~0.4 s, no bounce;
   Reduce Motion shortens travel, Dim Flashing Lights caps flashes.
8. On a phone the page sells a later Mac download (no dead download button on iOS).

## Bans
Gradient blur, spikes, particles, parallax, full-width hairlines, a second logo, melted
type, caps-mono microcopy, glass. Pink only as an overprint accent.
