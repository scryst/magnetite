# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MacBook users who listen to music while working and want playback controls to live in the otherwise unused space around the camera notch.

## Product Purpose

Turn the physical notch into a compact, always-available music surface. Retracted, it belongs entirely to the menu-bar band. Expanded, it becomes a polished player whose black ferrofluid mass visibly responds to real system audio and then coherently returns to the notch.

## Brand Personality

Alive, magnetic, visceral. The motion should feel like one intelligent, high-viscosity organism: expressive enough to be memorable, controlled enough to remain a useful native Mac utility.

## Anti-references

- Mirrored equalizer patterns or same-height spike combs.
- Sine-wave, spring, bounce, or other visibly periodic motion.
- Patchy layers of unrelated bubbles, circles, cones, and gradients.
- Detached balls or droplets floating over the player.
- A player that remains extended below the menu bar when it is not in use.

## Design Principles

- One continuous reservoir: its outline warps under the music and every peak remains rooted in that same surface.
- Audio causes events; a clock only advances the simulation.
- Expression changes which frequencies break the surface, how far they pull it, and how neighboring peaks share the displacement.
- Hardware first: never draw through the camera cutout, and never cover window chrome while retracted.
- Native utility, signature moment: controls stay familiar while the ferrofluid earns the spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast and native control semantics. Never replace the interface with an inert or ambiguous state: every state change must still read, and a control that renders must do something.
